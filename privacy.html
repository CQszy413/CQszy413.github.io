AAssociate AI

Privacy Policy

Effective August 25, 2026

Associate AI is a private testing workspace for reviewing and resolving state and local tax notices. Access to the application requires a workspace password and a separate Google authorization.

Google data we access

After you authorize Google, Associate AI may access Gmail messages and attachments in the configured intake scope and may read Drive files needed for a matched case. It may create case folders, reviewed drafts, and other files that the application owns. Sending email or creating an external communication requires the applicable in-product review and approval.

How data is used

Google data is used only to provide the requested notice-intake, document review, evidence search, drafting, and workflow functions. Relevant document content may be sent to the configured AI processing provider to perform those functions. Associate AI does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Associate AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and isolation

Each Google account is bound to an isolated Associate AI testing workspace. Application records and encrypted authorization material are stored in the configured database and object-storage providers. The private owner workspace is not visible to other testers.

Retention and deletion

The Reset test workspace control removes synced copies, case records, model outputs, and the stored Google authorization from that Associate AI workspace. It does not delete the original Gmail messages or Drive files. You may also request deletion through the support contact shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.

Your control

You can revoke Associate AI access at any time from your Google Account security settings. Revocation stops future access; use the workspace reset or the OAuth support contact to request deletion of previously stored testing data.
