AAssociate AI

Resolve tax notices with reviewable evidence.

Associate AI is a limited-access workspace for state and local tax professionals. It helps authorized users intake notices, inspect supporting documents, reconcile agency claims, and prepare reviewer-controlled next actions.

Open secure workspace Read privacy policy

Notice intake

With the user's permission, Associate AI reads messages and attachments in the configured Gmail intake scope and creates an isolated case for that user.

Evidence review

It searches the relevant Google Drive branch for returns, e-file acknowledgments, payment records, and workpapers needed to test the notice claim.

Controlled execution

It can prepare drafts and supporting files, while external communications remain subject to the review and approval controls shown in the workspace.

Data access and control

Google access is optional and begins only after the user completes Google's authorization flow. Each connected Google account is bound to a separate Associate AI testing workspace. Associate AI does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Users can revoke access from their Google Account and can remove stored testing data through the workspace controls.
